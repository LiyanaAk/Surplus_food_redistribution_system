How FoodShare Works

A simple, transparent way to rescue food and feed communities

1

Post Surplus Food

Hotels, restaurants, bakeries, or households list extra fresh food by providing quantity, safe consumption time, pickup address, and confirming that the food has been safely prepared.

2

Instant Priority & Alerts

Food with less remaining safe time is automatically tagged as Urgent or High Priority, notifying nearby volunteers so it gets collected quickly.

3

Volunteer Claims & Collects

Approved volunteers check the map, claim the food, and travel to the pickup address to collect the containers.

4

Safe Delivery to People in Need

The food is handed over to a registered shelter, orphanage, or distributed directly to people in need, logging meals served so donors can track their real impact.

How It Works

A simple way to get food to people who need it

1. Post Food

Donors list surplus meals with quantity, safe consumption time, and pickup address.

2. Quick Priority

Items with short expiry times get highlighted so nearby volunteers can pick them up quickly.

3. Volunteer Claims

Verified volunteers claim the food on the map and coordinate pickup immediately.

4. Safe Delivery

Food is delivered to shelters, orphanages, or distributed directly to people in need.

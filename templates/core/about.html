Our Mission

Share Food. Reduce Waste. Feed Communities.

Every day, good food goes to waste while people nearby go hungry. FoodShare connects those who have surplus food with volunteers and shelters who can put it to good use right away.

Fight Hunger

Direct fresh meals to orphanages, elderly care homes, and homeless individuals in our neighborhoods.

Stop Food Waste

Rescue edible food from restaurants, banquet halls, and kitchens before it ever reaches a landfill.

Unite Communities

Empower local volunteers and donors to work hand-in-hand with verified care institutions.

Why Real-Time Coordination Matters

Cooked food has a short shelf life. When events end or kitchens close, meals must be collected within hours. Our platform highlights urgent donations and shows volunteers exactly where food is waiting, making pickups fast, safe, and reliable.

Want to help make a difference?

Whether you have extra meals to share or time to pick up food, your involvement matters.
